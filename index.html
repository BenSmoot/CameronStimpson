Control

The final installment of the “Impenetrable” series follows Nevissi as she tackles a world where a previously annihilated power is released once again. The Author

About Cameron

Cameron Stimpson

Former Journalist · Current Novelist · Always a storyteller

Cameron Stimpson is a former journalist with published works on KSL.com, Deseret News, The Daily Universe, 50 Shades of Awkward, Supermoney, and Best Reviews. She has been writing novels since her mother taught her how to type at age six and is excited to start her publishing journey. She loves writing fantastical stories that explore themes of found families, empowering victims, and leave you rooting for the underdog.

When not writing, Cameron can be found traveling abroad reading fantasy and historical fiction, playing video games, swimming, and spending time with her pets, nieces, and nephews.
